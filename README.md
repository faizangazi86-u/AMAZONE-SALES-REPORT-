# Amazon Store Sales Analytics — Power BI Dashboard

> End-to-end sales analysis on **5,901 transactions** across 3 product categories, 4 regions, and 3 customer segments — built in Power BI.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of orders but rarely know:
- Which categories *look* profitable but are actually losing margin?
- Which customer segment drives the most revenue?
- Which regions are underperforming?

This dashboard answers all three.

---

## Key Findings

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Office Supplies = #1 in sales | $643K — 41% of total revenue |
| Tables sub-category loses money | -$11,091 profit despite consistent orders |
| Copiers = highest profit | $42,774 — most valuable sub-category |
| West region dominates | $522K — 33% of total revenue |
| Consumer segment leads | $753K — 48% of total sales |
| COD = most used payment | 41.6% of transactions |
